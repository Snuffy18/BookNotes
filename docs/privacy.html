BookNotes Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

BookNotes keeps your reading on your iPhone. The only things that leave your device are the page photos you choose to scan and the book details used to look up a book.

What stays on your device

Your library, notes, reports, reading sessions, streaks, goals and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. BookNotes has no accounts, and we do not keep a copy of this data on our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Page photos you scan

When you scan a page, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and passed to OpenAI, which reads the text and writes your notes. The result comes back to your device. We do not store your photos or the generated notes on our server.

OpenAI processes this data on our behalf under its API data usage policies. It does not use API data to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring before deleting it. Please don't scan pages that contain personal information you don't want processed this way.

Book lookups

When you add a book by scanning its barcode or searching, the ISBN or search text is sent to Open Library to fetch the title, author, cover and page count. Open Library's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Camera and photos

BookNotes uses the camera only to scan pages, covers and barcodes, and the photo library only when you pick an image. You can change these permissions any time in the Settings app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells us only whether a subscription is active.

What we don't do

BookNotes has no advertising, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell or share your data.

Children

BookNotes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you can view or delete it at any time in the app or by deleting the app. If you have questions or want to exercise your rights under laws such as the GDPR, email SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
