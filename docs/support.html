Common questions

The scan didn't pick up the page properly.

Lay the book flat in good light, fill the frame with the page and keep the text in focus. You can crop the photo before extracting. Handwriting and very small print may not read well.

Which languages are supported?

BookNotes can read printed pages in most languages. Notes are currently written in English.

Where are my notes stored?

Your library, notes and reading history are stored on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for what is sent off the device when you scan.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose BookNotes. You can cancel any time; access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open BookNotes, go to Profile and tap Restore purchase.
